# No Black Box: Full Visibility & Control

**Codie operates with complete transparency, showing you every file read and every proposed diff.**

Understand exactly what Codie is doing and why. Review all actions and approve changes before they're made. Codie uses checkpoints, allowing you to easily revert if needed, maintaining full control over your codebase. Model-source usage stays visible throughout the task.
